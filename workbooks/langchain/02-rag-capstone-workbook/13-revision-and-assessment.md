# W-RAG-13 - Defend the build and clean up

**Which claims can your evidence actually support?**

> **Your goal:** Assess essential dimensions, repair failed gates and preserve evidence before cleanup.<br>
> **You need:** [Independent recall extension](12-capstone-challenges.md), attempted tests and disclosed assistance.<br>
> **Effort:** Review/defense time belongs within the module reflection/independent allocation; no separate validated estimate.<br>
> **You will produce:** A dimension-by-dimension evidence record and honestly labelled modification result. **Status:** Draft; offline synthetic reference, unpiloted.

Use these public module criteria for self-assessment; they do not replace existing program rubrics or establish certification. Each dimension needs concrete evidence: reproducible setup; faithful parsing/provenance; justified retrieval choice; trusted identity/tenant/role enforcement; quote support and honest abstention; freshness/deletion and race tests; separate evaluation denominators; incident diagnosis and operational limits; independent recall integration and individual explanation.

**Review workflow:** collect evidence first, mark each dimension demonstrated/partial/missing, inspect critical gates separately, then choose the next repair. For each dimension record the evidence path, command/environment, observed property, assistance and remaining limit. Keep the original failing result beside the repaired result.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Public reference results and self-scoring are learning evidence. Independently reviewed ownership requires a reviewer and a genuinely unseen change; do not label self-selected practice as that review.

Critical gates are non-compensable: forbidden evidence exposure, unsupported returned claims, stale/deleted output after a tested invalidation, fabricated execution evidence or consequential side effects without authorization require repair regardless of other strengths. Mark each dimension demonstrated, partial or missing; module completion requires all dimensions demonstrated and no failed critical gate. This is formative guidance, not a new numeric certification score.

Submit source/lockfile versions, environment, commands and actual outputs, control tests, lexical/hybrid comparison, deletion inventory, incident note, independent code/tests/reports and disclosed AI assistance. Public reference execution proves the reference, not your ownership. Explain the final recheck and perform an unseen modification: change a recall withdrawal boundary or introduce an unfamiliar tenant before rerunning relevant tests.

For self-study, select a new variation and label it **self-directed transfer practice**. If a reviewer is available, let them select an unrehearsed modification and record who reviewed it and what was observed. If unavailable, mark independent review pending; do not fabricate a reviewer or an unseen result.

Reflection: distinguish authorization from similarity, citation existence from support, current revision from approved freshness, correct abstention from completed customer work, and index-row deletion from complete erasure. State explicitly that synthetic generation and authored embeddings provide no live-model quality or deployment evidence. Those remain program delivery requirements.

Cleanup from your private lab directory: preserve evidence privately, remove only results and .venv in that directory, and delete any temporary SQLite test database you deliberately retained. No cloud, model account or continuing cost is created. Return to the course map. Repeat a failed dimension with a different fixture; retain the first failure and repaired report rather than editing history.

Verify `Get-Location` is your intended private learner directory before cleanup. From that verified directory, `Remove-Item -LiteralPath .\results,.\.venv -Recurse -Force` removes only generated output/environment directories. Keep your changed source, tests and private submission artifacts.
