# Announcement: 2025 Benefits Updates

**Date:** 2025-01-05

Happy New Year! A few benefits changes take effect starting today, 2025-01-01:

## PTO Policy Update

The PTO accrual rate and year-end carryover cap have both increased under the new **2025 PTO Policy**. The maximum carryover is now **8 days** (up from 5), and accrual increases to 1.75 days/month. See the 2025 PTO Policy document for full details — it supersedes the 2024 version in its entirety.

## Remote Equipment Stipend Increase

The annual home-office equipment stipend for remote employees increases from **$500 to $600/year**, effective immediately. This updates the figure published in the Remote Work FAQ; the $600 amount is now current.

## Dental Coverage

Dental coverage now includes orthodontia for dependents under 18, at 50% coinsurance up to a $2,000 lifetime maximum.

Reach out to People Ops with any questions about how these changes affect you.
