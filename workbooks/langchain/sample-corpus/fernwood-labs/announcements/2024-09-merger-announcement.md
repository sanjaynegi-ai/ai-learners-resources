# Announcement: Fernwood Labs Acquires Atlas Vision

**Date:** 2024-09-16

We're excited to announce that Fernwood Labs has acquired **Atlas Vision**, a 12-person computer-vision startup based out of Austin, TX, known for its work on low-power edge inference models.

## What This Means

- Atlas Vision's team joins Fernwood Labs as the new **Edge AI** group, reporting to the VP of Engineering.
- Atlas Vision employees will formally onboard under Fernwood Labs' employee handbook, benefits, and policies starting **2024-10-01**.
- Atlas Vision's existing office lease will be wound down by end of Q1 2025; the team will transition to remote-first, following Fernwood's standard remote work policy.

## Timeline

- **2024-09-16:** Deal announced
- **2024-10-01:** Atlas Vision employees transition to Fernwood systems (Okta, Jamf, Jira, Slack) and begin accruing PTO under Fernwood's policy
- **Q1 2025:** Austin office lease concludes

Please join us in welcoming the Atlas Vision team. Questions about the integration can be directed to People Ops.
