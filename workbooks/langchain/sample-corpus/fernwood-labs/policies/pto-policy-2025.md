# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

**Effective Date:** 2025-01-01
**Status:** Current. This policy supersedes the PTO Policy effective 2024-01-01.

## What Changed

Effective January 1, 2025, the PTO accrual rate and carryover cap have both increased. This document replaces the 2024 PTO Policy in full. Where the two documents conflict, this 2025 policy governs.

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue PTO at a rate of **1.75 days per month**, equivalent to **21 days per year**. PTO begins accruing from the first day of employment but cannot be used until the 90-day probation period ends.

## Carryover

At the end of the calendar year, employees may carry forward a maximum of **8 days** of unused PTO into the next year (increased from 5 days under the 2024 policy). Carried-over days must be used by the end of Q1 of the new year or they are forfeited.

## Requesting Time Off

Requests for more than 3 consecutive days require manager approval submitted at least 2 weeks in advance. Requests for 1–2 days may be submitted with 48 hours' notice. (Unchanged from the 2024 policy.)

## Unused PTO at Termination

Employees are paid out for unused, accrued PTO at their final base salary rate upon termination, in accordance with local labor law. (Unchanged from the 2024 policy.)
