# Expense & Reimbursement Policy

**Effective Date:** 2023-03-01
**Status:** Current.

## Per Diem Rates

- Domestic travel: **$45/day** for meals and incidentals.
- International travel: **$65/day** for meals and incidentals.

## Receipts

A receipt is required for any single expense over **$25**. Expenses under $25 may be submitted without a receipt, up to a monthly cap of $100 in receipt-free claims.

## Mileage

Personal vehicle use for business purposes is reimbursed at **$0.67 per mile**, matching the current IRS standard mileage rate.

## Submission

All expenses must be submitted through Expensify within **30 days** of the expense date. Expenses submitted after 30 days require VP approval to be processed.

## Pre-Approval

Any single expense over $500, and all travel booked outside the standard Egencia portal, requires pre-approval from the employee's manager.
