# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Status:** Superseded — see `pto-policy-2025.md` for the current policy.

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue PTO at a rate of **1.5 days per month**, equivalent to **18 days per year**. PTO begins accruing from the first day of employment but cannot be used until the 90-day probation period ends.

## Carryover

At the end of the calendar year, employees may carry forward a maximum of **5 days** of unused PTO into the next year. Any unused PTO above this cap is forfeited on December 31st.

## Requesting Time Off

Requests for more than 3 consecutive days require manager approval submitted at least 2 weeks in advance. Requests for 1–2 days may be submitted with 48 hours' notice.

## Unused PTO at Termination

Employees are paid out for unused, accrued PTO at their final base salary rate upon termination, in accordance with local labor law.
