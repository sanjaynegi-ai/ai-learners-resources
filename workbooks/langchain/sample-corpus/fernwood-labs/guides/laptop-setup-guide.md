# New Hire Laptop Setup Guide

## Before Day One

IT ships your laptop pre-enrolled in **Jamf** (our MDM) to the address on file. It arrives sealed with a setup card.

## First Boot

1. Unbox and power on. The Jamf enrollment profile installs automatically on first network connection.
2. Sign in with your **Okta** single sign-on credentials (sent separately by People Ops before your start date).
3. The standard software bundle installs automatically: Slack, Zoom, 1Password, VS Code, and the company VPN client.
4. Set up 1Password and import your starter vault, shared by your manager.

## Requesting Additional Software

Software not in the standard bundle (e.g., design tools, specialized IDEs) must be requested via an IT ticket in the `ITOPS` Jira project. Most requests are approved within 2 business days; anything with a license cost over $200/year requires manager approval first.

## Returning Equipment

Laptops and peripherals must be returned to IT within 5 business days of an employee's last day. A $300 non-return fee applies after that window.
