# Remote Work FAQ

**Last Updated:** 2023-06-01

**Q: Who approves a remote work arrangement?**
A: Your manager and People Ops must both approve. Submit the request through the People Ops portal at least 2 weeks before your intended start date.

**Q: What are the core hours I need to be available?**
A: 10:00 AM–4:00 PM in your local time zone, matching the core collaboration hours in the employee handbook.

**Q: Does Fernwood Labs provide an equipment stipend for remote employees?**
A: Yes — remote employees receive a **$500/year** equipment stipend for home-office setup (desk, chair, monitor, etc.), reimbursed through Expensify.

**Q: Can I work remotely from another country?**
A: International remote work requires a separate legal and tax review and is capped at **30 days per calendar year** without a formal relocation. Contact People Ops before booking any international remote work.

**Q: Do I need a company-managed device to work remotely?**
A: Yes. Remote work must be performed on a company-managed laptop with the standard security software installed, connected via VPN when accessing internal systems.
