# Travel FAQ

**Q: How do I book business travel?**
A: All flights and hotels must be booked through the **Egencia** portal using your corporate login. Bookings outside Egencia require manager pre-approval (see the Expense Policy).

**Q: What's the per diem for meals while traveling?**
A: See the Expense Policy — $45/day domestic, $65/day international.

**Q: Do I need approval for international travel?**
A: Yes, all international travel requires **VP-level approval**, submitted at least 3 weeks in advance to allow time for visa processing if needed.

**Q: Are visa costs reimbursed?**
A: Yes, visa application fees for approved business travel are fully reimbursed through Expensify. Attach the visa receipt and the travel approval email to your expense report.

**Q: What if my flight is delayed and I miss a connection?**
A: Contact the Egencia 24/7 travel support line first; they can rebook you directly. Any additional costs from the rebooking are reimbursable as normal travel expenses.
