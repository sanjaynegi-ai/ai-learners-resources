# Fernwood Labs Employee Handbook

**Last Updated:** 2023-06-01

## Our Values

Fernwood Labs operates by three core values:

1. **Curiosity** — we ask why before we ask how.
2. **Candor** — we give feedback directly and kindly.
3. **Craft** — we take pride in the quality of what we ship.

## Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM local time, with **core collaboration hours of 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM** during which employees are expected to be reachable for meetings. Outside core hours, employees may flex their schedule with manager agreement.

## Probation Period

All new hires serve a **90-day probation period**, during which either party may end employment with 1 week's notice. Benefits (including PTO accrual eligibility for use, though not accrual itself) begin after probation ends.

## Code of Conduct

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation of any kind are prohibited and should be reported to People Ops at people-ops@fernwoodlabs.example or via the anonymous ethics hotline.

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in June and December. Each review includes self-assessment, manager assessment, and peer feedback from at least two colleagues.
